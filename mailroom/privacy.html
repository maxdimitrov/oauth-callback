Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026. Applies to mailroom and the owner's local Gmail tools that share its Google OAuth client.

Who uses it

Only the owner, on Google accounts the owner controls. No other users are accepted.

What data is accessed

Through the Gmail API: message headers, message bodies, labels and filters of the authorized accounts. The scopes requested are gmail.modify and gmail.settings.basic. The tools cannot permanently delete mail.

How it is used

To label, archive and summarize the owner's own mail. A short excerpt of a message may be sent to Anthropic's Claude model to classify it, under the owner's own Claude account.

Where it is stored

OAuth tokens and a log of the actions taken are stored on the owner's own computers. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Contact: maxa252@gmail.com
